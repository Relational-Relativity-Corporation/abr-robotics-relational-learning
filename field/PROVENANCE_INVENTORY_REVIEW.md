# Provenance inventory — connected three-locus signal field

**Status: DECLARED candidate organization; numerical M, execution and verification OPEN.** Origin authorizes this revised inventory of one functioning organism: LiDAR, motors and genetics participate in the same connected relational process. This is not a specification for three independently programmed behaviors. The six relationships below replace the previous four-node/twelve-edge inventory **in this document only**. They are not a claim that executable declarations, routing or Kernel inputs have already been migrated.

## Participating loci and physical context

- **N2 — LiDAR:** digitized environmental observations, including the bearing and range of each actual return.
- **N3 — Motors:** left and right forward-only binary actuators, retaining each channel's command record and independent observed response.
- **N4 — Genetics:** the inherited SEEK predisposition and the declared stopping clearance of **0.15 m from the robot's front surface**. This is a disposition of the organism, not a separately calculated genetic controller.
- **Robot body:** physical geometry, sensor mounting and measurement context. It is **not an independently measured active node** in this revised field. The existing 0.12 m sensor-to-front mounting offset remains contextual geometry; it is not a new sensor observation or an extra locus.

N2, N3 and N4 form one connected relational field through exactly the six directed relationships below. Reverse directions carry their own declared source information and provenance; an arrow does not establish a numerical value by itself.

## Field-wide provenance and observation requirements

1. **Entry into the field.** Each signal must retain its originating record/device or simulator, locus, channel, quantity, native unit, digital representation, reference frame, acquisition conditions, time/progression and validity. Configuration, inherited instruction, command, measured feedback and calculation must remain distinguishable. Missing or invalid information stays unavailable; it is not fabricated or padded with zero.
2. **Propagation through the connected organism.** Changes at each locus must be available through its declared outgoing relationships, preserving source identity and progression at the receiving locus. The inventory describes the connected provenance needed for one organism's disposition, observation and physical response. **Packet delivery is not demonstrated mathematical propagation.** Demonstration requires traceable source changes entering declared M, canonical whole-field outputs and subsequent observations showing the actual response.
3. **One canonical calculation.** A, B, R and rho apply together to the **whole admitted three-locus field**, not to separate genetic, sensing, steering or motor computations. Spatial reciprocal relationships are distinct from temporal wraparound. Actual preceding observations establish persistence; no fabricated prior or reset substitutes for field continuity. This document does not assert that the revised candidate field is already admitted or executable.
4. **Heterogeneous measurements remain heterogeneous.** Physical return amplitude, range in m, angular position/bearing in rad, angular velocity in rad/s, encoder counts, actuator commands and digital genetic disposition are different quantities. A common JSON number representation does not make them commensurate. Preserve their native units and roles; do not invent normalization, signal strength, numeric genetic encoding, synthetic measurements or conversions.
5. **Physical context and response.** Sensor-origin range and front-surface clearance have distinct references. Mounting geometry must accompany any declared clearance interpretation; the 0.15 m clearance objective does not change the LiDAR's 0.15 m sensor-origin minimum range. Simulator geometry may generate observations and independently evaluate behavior, but is not a substitute for component observations. Successful computation or command transmission establishes neither motion nor genetically directed seeking. Actual response requires subsequent independently recorded component observations.
6. **Acquisition and change validity.** Declare matching of returns, independent motor sample times, command issue times, observation progression and asynchronous/missing-data treatment. No-return/invalid samples remain in provenance. Successive changes must identify their input records and any discarded information; they must not replace canonical A, B, R or rho with frame-difference behavior.

## Inspection baseline and current implementation limits

The remote inventory inspected at `90e8207` describes the earlier executable four-node/twelve-edge projection. Its published configuration still carries the historical 0.10 m target. The inspected **uncommitted local** implementation contains binary actuator interfaces, independent feedback, per-beam acquisition records, full instruction readback and the Origin-updated 0.15 m target. Those local source changes are not published by this Markdown-only revision and are not evidence of physical hardware capabilities.

- `robot/substrate.py::scan()` supplies simulated `range_m`, `bearing_rad`, `acquisition_time_s`, sample index and Boolean validity. It supplies **no intensity**, quality/amplitude measurement or hardware accuracy model.
- `robot/substrate.py::motor_packet()` retains independent left/right encoder angles, shaft velocities, binary command states and simulated applied drive torque. A common acquisition time and simulated origin are recorded. Physical encoder counts/resolution and hardware electrical measurements are not established by that simulator.
- `robot/signals.py::InstructionMemory` persists and reads back a JSON instruction and its SHA-256. It does **not establish inheritance**. The separate legacy phototaxis inheritance implementation does not establish inherited SEEK provenance for this revised field.
- `robot/run_experiment.py::RobotObserver` routes component packets in the **old twelve-edge graph**. Its numerical input is still reduced by `node_values()` to body offset, forward range, **mean** wheel encoder arc and the target clearance. Bearing, independent motor channels, binary states and full SEEK disposition are not numerical Kernel inputs. No valid forward return means no Kernel call, even if other bearings contain returns.
- `robot/kernel_bridge/src/main.rs` still constructs four nodes, one numerical component and no component pairs, then calls canonical `execute_spatial` / `execute_with_prior`. This is real canonical execution of the old projection, **not execution or verification of these six candidate relationships**. Its diagnostic path does not itself establish observational admission. The body-offset node must not be mistaken for the revised body-as-context declaration.

## Status and OPEN conditions for Origin review

**DECLARED** means Origin has specified a locus, directed relationship, disposition or measurement context; it does not imply measured numerical values. **OBSERVED** means an actually acquired signal/readback, explicitly labelled simulated or physical. **CALCULATED** means an identified transformation or canonical output with its inputs preserved. **VERIFIED** requires a bounded comparison against specified evidence and criteria; computation alone is not verification. **OPEN** means required evidence or a declaration is absent. These distinctions apply per signal and per relationship; the revised candidate field is not labelled VERIFIED.

The following remain **OPEN**:

- LiDAR intensity/amplitude availability and calibration; hardware quantization, uncertainty and encoder count characteristics where not acquired.
- Proven association and progression of successive returns and motor feedback; numerical representation of the declared changes without inventing measurements.
- An inherited, versioned SEEK record with parent/source/readback evidence, and its exact digital-to-numerical M representation.
- A whole-field M preserving range, bearing and independent motor/genetic information with heterogeneous units and explicit correspondence. Kernel V9's existing input requires a value for every declared component at every locus; neither ragged input nor arbitrary padding resolves missing correspondence. Required component pairs and coupling values, if any, must be declared rather than invented.
- Migration of the executable four-node/twelve-edge declaration, router and bridge to the revised three-locus/six-edge field, **only under separate authorization**. This task changes none of them.
- An Origin-authorized interpretation of whole-field canonical outputs into left/right binary actuator states. No steering, approach/stop or genetic-to-motor rule is introduced here.
- Demonstrated mathematical propagation and observed physical response across the revised connected field. Prior motor-interface tests or old-topology Kernel results do not verify this candidate topology.

Only this provenance Markdown inventory is revised. Executable code, other declarations, physical behavior, canonical Kernel mathematics and all historical evidence remain unchanged.
